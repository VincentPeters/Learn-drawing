  - "Where two solids touch or intersect, deepen the crevice between them."

checkpoint: "Objects sit on the surface instead of floating above it."

Ambient light reaches everywhere except the tight gaps where surfaces nearly touch. Those crevices are the darkest values in the whole drawing — spend them there.
