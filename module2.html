Module 2 · the artisan's layer

Rendering, shading & colour

Module 1 gave you precise, correct line work. This module adds what turns a technical drawing into a plate: tone that follows the form, one convincing light, and the graphite-plus-blue-and-sanguine colour of the academic constructive tradition. 15 lessons, same daily rhythm.

Before you start: you'll get the most from this if you're comfortable with construction and ellipses from Module 1 (through about Day 15). You don't need to have finished all 30 days — just be able to build a solid you can then render.
